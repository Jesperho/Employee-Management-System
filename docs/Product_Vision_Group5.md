# Product Vision: Employee Management System

**Group #5**  
**Date:** 16/01/2025

---

## Problem Statement

Managing employee information using spreadsheets often leads to problems such as data loss, duplication, difficulty locating specific employees, and inefficiency when handling large amounts of information. As companies grow, Excel-based management becomes complex and time-consuming.

## Solution

Our Employee Management App addresses these challenges by providing a structured, centralized, and user-friendly application that simplifies employee data management. The product transforms the large information into a clear, searchable, and well-organized system, allowing companies to access employee data quickly and reliably.

## Target Audience

The application is designed for organizations of all sizes, from small businesses to large ones, that struggles to find an efficient way to manage employees, departments, leave, and salaries. By replacing manual processes with a digital solution, the system improves accuracy, productivity, and overall efficiency.

## Key Features

The Employee Management System includes the following core functionalities:

- Five main views: Home, Employee, Department, Leave, and Salary
- Filtering by department, position, status, and other attributes
- Fast employee search by name
- Department-specific reports and insights
- Overview of all employees currently on leave
- Salary management and tracking for all employees

## Goals and Objective

The primary goals of this project are to:

- Help companies manage employees efficiently and reliably
- Reduce time spent on manual searches and data handling
- Provide clear reports to improve departmental performance
- Track salaries accurately and calculate total monthly salary expenses

## Vision

Our vision is to build a fully functional, intuitive, and efficient employee management application that saves companies time and effort while improving data visibility and organization. The product enables businesses to focus on strategic tasks rather than administrative work. Ultimately, this leads to better task allocation, improved productivity, and potential growth in company performance and reputation.
